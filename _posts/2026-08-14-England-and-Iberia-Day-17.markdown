Our final full day on the ship, and a sea day. This will be brief.

Our adjoining staterooms. Rowan and I were in the left one, Sherri and Siena on the right.

There was a heavy set of doors that ran between the rooms. As far as we could tell, there wasn't a way to prop the doors open. The utility of having a door between the rooms was marginal.
