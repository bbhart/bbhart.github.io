- **End of day:** At sea

Welcome to Málaga, Spain!

We docked in Málaga at 07:00 with all aboard at 17:00 -- a long day in port, and after Ibiza we were glad to have the morning rather than waiting out the afternoon heat. I'd booked just Sherri and me for a walking and eating shore excursion from 9:30am to 12:30pm. Did we beat the heat? Sort of!

[Málaga Cathedral](https://malagacatedral.com/cultural-visit/) is the obvious landmark, and the [Museo Picasso](https://www.museopicassomalaga.org/en) is a few streets on from it. Picasso was born here. Had we more time...

The best view of the day was from up at the [Gibralfaro](https://alcazabaygibralfaro.malaga.eu/en/), looking back down over the bullring, the city and the whole port -- our ship included.

Our shore excursion dropped us back at the ship at around 1:00pm.

We grabbed Siena (Rowan wasn't interested) and proceeded to walk from the ship back into town and [Calle Larios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calle_Marqu%C3%A9s_de_Larios), which takes around 20 minutes, most of it along the harbor.

We stopped for lunch at [Sushita Chinitas](https://www.sushita.com/sushita-chinitas/), just off Calle Larios. It was advertised as a sushi restaurant (though you'd never guess it from the decorating).

We did some strolling and shopping, then meandered our way back towards the ship, cutting past the [Centre Pompidou](https://www.centrepompidou-malaga.eu/en/)'s glass cube.

We sailed at 17:00, and the run west put us back through the Strait of Gibraltar in the evening -- the second time this trip, and this time in great light.

Reflecting back on the overall cruise here two weeks later (I'm writing this on August 24), I feel Málaga was my favorite stop on the trip. The views and scenery were incredible, the streets and parks well-kept, the local people we met were exceedingly friendly, and it's on the mainland so it could serve as a base for visiting other places. I could see myself spending several weeks here, though perhaps not in August!
