On sea days they open the [Eden Cafe](https://www.celebritycruises.com/things-to-do-onboard/eat-and-drink/restaurants-and-cafes/eden-cafe), which I enjoy for their Build Your Own salads and the outdoor sitting area:

White sail-like shade canopies stretch overhead, white woven lounge chairs line the teak deck, a man in a blue shirt sits at a table further along, and open ocean is visible past the glass railing at right" width="100%" />

I dined at the solo travelers' table in the evening and treated myself to the Coquilles Saint-Jacques, a favorite from our August cruise.
