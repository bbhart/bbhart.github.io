My data service wasn't working today. This meant Google Maps wasn't working[^1] so I couldn't get directions to any of the places on my cheat sheet (the [mercado](https://www.cm-matosinhos.pt/pages/426?poi_id=8062), probably a cathedral or some sort of scenic overlook, I don't remember). We found a printed tourist map that helped a little. (After a few hours I eventually discovered my phone's VPN was glitching -- connected but showing not connected, but by that point a lot of wind had gone out of my sails.)

We didn't have a plan for today anyway. Porto was further away using transit than we wanted to deal with, we were still lethargic from the jet lag, and the weather was gray with occasional misting rain. We explored the town at the port, Matosinhos, walking up and back down a main street.

Rowan and Sherri returned to the ship, as I wanted a local lunch and they weren't keen. Siena, usually up for an adventure, tagged along with me. We found a restaurant near the cruise terminal entrance. Unfortunately, my glitching cell service meant I couldn't check reviews first, and this one was not a winner. The service wasn't especially friendly, and they'd somehow run out of the cured ham listed on their starter menu[^2]. I'm not exaggerating when I say prices for anything beyond the starters were either €16 or €112, without much in between. We had bread and olives and made our way back toward the ship.

Overall, as you might be able to tell, we just weren't feeling it. In hindsight we should've done Porto, of course. This port felt like a lackluster start to the cruise. So it goes.
