- **Start of day:** Palma de Mallorca, Spain
- **End of day:** At sea

It's hot. 

We started our day with a walk from the ship up to the castle on the hill, [Castell de Bellver](https://castelldebellver.palma.es/en/).

The heat was oppressive, truly, and we were grateful they weren't gouging on water at the castle. I should have brought water. I didn't. We bought the kids water in town, but I figured I'd tough it out. It was a mistake.

I toured the castle, didn't enjoy it much on account of said lack of water, and as far as I could tell there was no water fountain inside the castle on any of its three floors. I did briefly consider drinking from the bathroom sink faucet. There is a bar/restaurant outside the ticketed area, but I didn't want to take the chance there was no re-entry. We grabbed bottled water here after the castle and sat for a few minutes.

With an hour until our arranged excursion and an hour's walk in front of us (time got away from me at the castle), we started back down the hill. Would I have liked to summon a taxi from the top? Very much so. However, a recommended taxi app for Palma would not let me book a cab without a credit card on file (even when I indicated I'd pay the driver cash), and the app refused both a VISA credit card I've used worldwide and a debit card. The ticket office helpfully has a sign posted with the taxi company's dispatch number, but I don't buy voice service for these international trips.

We took paths down the hill into the edge of town and were then fortunate to find an idling taxi just a few meters from where we emerged from the woods. It was our destiny to take this cab. The driver cut a 50-minute walk down to a 15-minute, €8 ride with air conditioning, and got us to our tour with ample time.

Our excursion, booked through [GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com?partner_id=S80Z3R3&cmp=share_to_earn)[^affiliate] (my first time trying that platform), was a two-hour Segway tour around the old town. It was great! The midday heat was relentless, and I doubt we would have seen even a twentieth of what we saw had we been on foot. Rowan did get the dizzies at one point, which we managed with sunglasses, adequate water, a Coke, and ice cream. I have some great GoPro footage of a lot of it, which I'll cut into a proper video at some point.

After the tour I talked everyone into tapas at [Maura](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187463-d21133870-Reviews-Maura_Cafe_Bar-Palma_de_Mallorca_Majorca_Balearic_Islands.html), which was OK, and then we taxi'd back to the ship. Twice now I've tried to treat the family to tapas in hopes of building a tradition, and both times the food has been just OK -- nowhere near tapas I've had in other countries, like a place my Dad and I found in Basel, Switzerland, or just The Columbia in Celebration. I'll make a few more attempts.
