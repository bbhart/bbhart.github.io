We were seeking out [El Salón de Mi Casa](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g187454-d25185395-Reviews-El_Salon_De_Mi_Casa_Cat_Cafe-Bilbao_Province_of_Vizcaya_Basque_Country.html), a cat café in Bilbao. They said reservations were recommended but not required. We showed up, rang the bell, and nobody came. We peeked in windows but we saw people nor cats. 

Instead we ended up going to McDonalds for one child, and then Taco Bell for the other. The Taco Bell happened to have the fastest WiFi I’ve ever encountered in any fast food restaurant anywhere, allowing me to do some backups and reduce my anxiety somewhat.

Later, leaving Bilbao, we passed this breakwater lighthouse. My memory of last time we passed it was that there was lots of people there to wave us off. 

<img src="/assets/20260813-getxo-breakwater-lighthouse.jpg" alt="A white stone lighthouse at the end of a long breakwater, seen from a departing ship. It stands on a massive semicircular stone bastion whose lower courses are stained green at the waterline. I checked my old photos and, sure enough, we had a huge send-off in 2007. I'm guessing the novelty of large ships in Bilbao has worn off now.

<img src="/assets/20260813-getxo-lighthouse-2007-vs-2026.jpg" alt="A two-panel side-by-side comparison of the same breakwater lighthouse nineteen years apart, each panel headed by a black bar with the year in white. The right panel, labelled 2026, shows the same tower and bastion from a similar angle in bright summer light, now with coloured graffiti sprayed across the base of the tower and the small building beside it, only a scattering of people on the bastion, and the mole running away to the right past a white railing." width="100%" />

We could've done more in Bilbao. I think we're at the point in the cruise where we're starting to get travel burnout and just weren't feeling it. I'd like to do a proper visit sometime, including the Guggenheim.
