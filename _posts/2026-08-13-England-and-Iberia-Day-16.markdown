They said reservations were recommended but not required. We showed up, rang the bell, and nobody came. Instead we ended up going to McDonalds and then Taco Bell (which had the fastest WiFi I’ve ever encountered in any fast food restaurant anywhere) and then back to the bus. On the bus back to the ship we were in front of a man who was hacking up a lung and shouldn’t have been out of bed much less in 90 degree heat or an 80 degree bus packed with others.
