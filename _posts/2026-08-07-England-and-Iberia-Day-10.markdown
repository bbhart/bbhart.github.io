- **Start of day:** Barcelona, Spain
- **End of day:** At sea

I started getting sick yesterday -- a sore throat and general blahs that I mostly attributed to dehydration. It progressively got worse while in Palma, and rehydrating (a lot) back on the ship didn't fix it. So I woke up on this Barcelona day feeling pretty wrecked: sore throat, slight aches, and very tired. Regardless, I knew I needed to make something of our time here.

I left the ship around 8:45am, solo, with a mission in this order: stroll La Rambla, find cold and flu medication and shaving gel, wander Mercat de la Boqueria before the full tourist onslaught, and have a bite of breakfast. I succeeded at all of it and was back on the ship by 11 or so. I do wish I'd been feeling more up to it, because I would have loved to wander away from the hardcore tourist areas and into the residential blocks.

Siena and Sherri left the ship later in the afternoon to have the La Rambla experience for themselves.

By the way, the La Rambla experience right now is... not good. There's major construction happening along a significant portion of it. I'm sure they're building toward something better, which is a worthy goal, but the experience today lacks the charm.
