- **Start of day:** Barcelona, Spain
- **End of day:** At sea

I started getting sick yesterday -- a sore throat and general blahs that I mostly attributed to dehydration. It progressively got worse while in Palma, and rehydrating (a lot) back on the ship didn't fix it. I woke up on this Barcelona port day feeling pretty wrecked: sore throat, slight aches, and very tired. Regardless, I knew I needed to make something of our time here.

I left the ship around 8:45am, solo, with a mission in this order: stroll La Rambla, find cold and flu medication and shaving gel, wander [Mercat de la Boqueria](https://www.boqueria.barcelona) before the full tourist onslaught, and have a bite of breakfast. I succeeded at all of it and was back on the ship by 11 or so, drenched in sweat and ready to be horizontal. I do wish I'd been feeling more up to it. Sherri and I had a terrific first visit [back in 2007]({% post_url 2007-05-04-europe-2007-day-15 %}) during our European honeymoon cruise. I would have loved to wander away from the hardcore tourist areas and explore the residential blocks, perhaps visit the now-finished [Sagrada Familia](https://sagradafamilia.org/en/), and maybe even do the hop-on/hop-off bus. 

Well, maybe not that last one. The queue for it at the port was wicked!

Siena and Sherri left the ship later in the afternoon to have the La Rambla experience for themselves.

By the way, the La Rambla experience right now is... not good. There's major construction happening along a significant portion of it. [They seem to be building toward something better](https://www.barcelona.cat/larambla/en/works), which is a worthy goal, but the experience today lacks the anticipated charm.
