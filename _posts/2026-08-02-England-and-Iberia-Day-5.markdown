- **Start of day:** At sea
- **End of day:** At sea

Sea day.

We all slept in, still working through the ravages of jet lag. I slept solid from 10pm to 8:30am or so, and yet my Apple Watch was convinced I'd had a bad night and was up a few times.

I had a light breakfast at the high-tops in the back of the buffet with Sherri, and did the same for lunch a bit later. I laid out for a while by myself, and then with Sherri and Siena.

Around 4:30 I went up to Eden to enjoy the view and update this log. We'll likely do 6pm dinners for the rest of this cruise. There's no waiting to get into a restaurant at that hour and the service is fast.
