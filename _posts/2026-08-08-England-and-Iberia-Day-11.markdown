The past three days -- Palma, Barcelona, Ibiza -- have been exceptionally warm. As in, drenched in sweat within a few minutes of walking away from the ship. Anticipating the heat, we didn't leave the ship to tour Ibiza until 5:00pm, and even that was too early with the benefit of hindsight.

I really enjoyed Ibiza. I couldn't quite put my finger on what it was like. Some combination of Carmel, California, and Key West and St. Augustine, Florida? I know I'm doing it an injustice, but it was unlike anywhere I've been before.

We took a shuttle from the port to the outskirts of the old town, then started strolling the streets. The shops were open, but we were still too early for the "proper" restaurants to open. As you walk along the waterfront you get more of the restaurants with photos of the food out front and someone trying to draw you in. Further up the hill, in the meandering side streets and narrow alleys, you find the more interesting establishments. We found a lovely Italian place called [Il Pavone](https://sites.google.com/view/ilpavonerestaurante) that was just opening.
