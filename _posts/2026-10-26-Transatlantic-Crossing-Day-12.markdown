- **End of day:** At sea

The ship was rocking! There was a lot of front-to-back movement (where you feel heavy for a few seconds and then light for a few seconds, over and over) as well as side-to-side sway. This being the third sea day in a row with a fair bit of motion I think my brain was over it. I was drowsy much of the day.

I tried to capture the ship motion. It's no easy thing.
