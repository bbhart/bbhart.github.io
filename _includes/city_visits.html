{%- comment -%} Visits to one _data/cities.yml entry, one per trip: shared by the home map (city_index.html) and the Places page (places.md). Call it inside a loop over site.data.cities, passing the loop's entry: include city_visits.html entry=entry kinds="stays" (home map) include city_visits.html entry=entry kinds="stays,day_trips" (Places) `entry` is one [name, data] pair from site.data.cities. `kinds` is a comma-separated list of the entry's visit fields to consider: `stays` (trip key to nights; 0 is a cruise port or shore excursion) and `day_trips` (list of trip keys). A key in both counts as a stay. A post is the trip's recap when its categories include "rollup" or its slug is the trip's _data/trips.yml key. Any other post of a trip with `start` and `end` counts only when its date (as %Y-%m-%d) is from `start` to `end` (or `posted_until` when set), inclusive, so planning and follow-up posts are skipped. Those in-window posts are the visit's day posts. Liquid has no return values, so this sets parallel arrays in the caller's scope, one element per visit, ordered by each trip's newest counted post tagging the city (newest first): visit_count number of visits (0 when no post matches) visit_keys trip key (rollup_key) visit_kinds "stays" or "day_trips" visit_nights stays[key] for a stay, nil for a day trip visit_posts chosen post: the trip's earliest day post tagging the city, else the recap visit_images chosen post's background, else the trip recap's, else nil visit_trips trip label and year from _data/trips.yml, else post title visit_day_counts number of day posts for the visit Read element i of each with visit_posts[i] and so on. Day posts come as one flat list across all visits, grouped by visit in the order above and oldest first within a visit, with a parallel list of the visit each belongs to: day_posts the day posts (never a recap) day_visits visit index i (a number) of day_posts[n] The day posts of visit i are the day_posts[n] where day_visits[n] == i.
